# Support Policies

Use these policies when responding to customer support tickets. Keep replies
clear, polite, and specific to the customer's issue.

## Delivery Delays

- If an order is late by fewer than 5 business days, apologize and ask the
  customer to monitor tracking for 24 to 48 hours.
- If tracking has not updated for more than 3 business days, offer to check
  with the carrier.
- If the order is more than 5 business days late, escalation to the shipping
  team may be needed.
- Always include the ticket ID when escalating a delivery issue.

## Refunds

- Unopened items can usually be refunded within 30 days of delivery.
- Opened items may require additional review before a refund is approved.
- Ask the customer to confirm the order number if it is missing.
- Refunds should be processed back to the original payment method.
- Escalate refund requests involving high-value items or policy exceptions.

## Damaged Products

- Apologize for the condition of the item.
- Ask the customer to keep the packaging until the case is resolved.
- Request photos of the damage if they have not already been provided.
- Offer a replacement or refund when the damage is confirmed.
- Escalate urgent replacement requests or safety-related damage.

## Payment Issues

- For duplicate charges, confirm that the billing team will review the payment.
- Ask the customer not to dispute the charge while the support review is active,
  unless they believe there is fraud.
- Temporary authorization holds may disappear within 3 to 5 business days.
- Escalate duplicate charges, failed refunds, suspected fraud, or missing
  payment confirmations to the billing team.

## Account Access

- Ask the customer to check spam or junk folders for password reset emails.
- Recommend trying the password reset process again after 10 minutes.
- Confirm that the customer is using the email address linked to the account.
- Escalate if reset emails never arrive, the account may be compromised, or the
  customer is locked out after multiple attempts.

## General Questions

- Answer the question directly when policy information is available.
- If the request is unclear, ask one focused follow-up question.
- Keep the tone friendly and concise.
- Escalation is usually not needed for general questions.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate High priority tickets when customer access, money, safety, or urgent
  delivery is at risk.
- Escalate Medium priority tickets when a specialist team must review the issue.
- Do not promise an exact resolution time unless the policy provides one.
- Tell the customer what will happen next when escalation is needed.
- Include the ticket ID and a short reason in any internal escalation note.
